Coniti Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 20, 2026

Coniti ("we", "the app") respects your privacy. This document explains what data we process when you use the Coniti mobile app, why, and who we share it with.

1. Who we are

Coniti is developed by Coniti. For any questions about privacy or data handling, contact us at epelzhenya@gmail.com.

3. What we do NOT collect

  • Your name, email, or phone number — no registration is required; the app uses anonymous authentication.
  • Recordings of your voice — audio is never saved by the app, only briefly processed to convert it to text.
  • Your precise location — the app never asks for that permission.

4. Third parties we share data with

  • Supabase — database hosting and server-side logic (Edge Functions).
  • Anthropic (Claude API) — analyzes your transcript text for AI grading and quiz questions, and processes photos/PDFs you upload and the learning goal you describe (Pro users only).
  • RevenueCat — manages subscriptions and purchases.
  • Google AdMob — shows ads to free users.
  • Apple / Google — on-device speech recognition provided by the operating system.

We do not sell your data to third parties.

5. Data retention and deletion

Your attempt history is stored locally on your device and fully under your control — you can clear it at any time in the app's Settings ("Clear attempt history"), or by deleting the app.

To request deletion of data tied to your device's anonymous ID on our server (subscription status, etc.), contact us at epelzhenya@gmail.com — we'll remove it within a reasonable time.

6. Children

The app is not intended for children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect data from users in that age group.

7. Changes to this policy

We may update this document from time to time. The current version is always available at this same link; the update date is shown at the top of the page.
